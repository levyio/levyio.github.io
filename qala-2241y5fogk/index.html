Qālā

qālā — Aramaic: voice, sound

Sound bends. It curves around corners because its waves are the size of a room, and a layer of warm air will steer it back to the ground. It can deflect a laser beam. Light, absorbed, can make a sound. Both move matter — and on a plate, sound sorts loose grains into a figure you can photograph.

This instrument plays that last one, and leaves the rest open.
