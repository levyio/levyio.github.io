Qālā. The mixer is fed back on itself — the sound is the electricity of this circuit, nothing else. The mic puts your room inside the loop; the camera or an image puts a picture into the fusion, repainted by the grains and torn by the sound. Capture for print opens a 300 dpi canvas and draws your whole take into it as one long exposure; Save the print writes the file, with the patch that made it stored inside.
