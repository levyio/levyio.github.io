    What this is

    The gasket's Laplacian spectrum is not solved numerically and then trusted — it is generated by a quadratic recursion, so the overtone series is exact in a way no ordinary drum's is. The eigenvalues here are computed twice, by two unrelated routes, and the page refuses to claim agreement it has not checked.

    A Koch drum's eigenfunctions were drawn in 1996. As far as six searches could establish, a fractal drum's actual eigenvalues have not been played. Absence of a search result is weak evidence, so treat that as a claim about what we could find, not about what exists. See docs/PRIOR-ART.md.

    Convention: interior vertices have degree 4, corners are clamped, and M = 4I − A. Elsewhere you will see λ(5 − 4λ) for the normalised Laplacian, whose eigenvalues are these divided by four. Same relation, different scaling.
