Roughness is not fractality

Control · the one that says no

Takagi's function is continuous, nowhere differentiable, visibly rough — and its dimension is exactly 1. It is not a fractal. Every instrument above can be wrong, and this is the one that proves the measurement can tell.

What links them. All four are mode selection: a system is driven, and out of everything it could do it picks one shape at one scale.

A Chladni plate does it linearly, inside a boundary, and the figure holds still. The gasket does it on a domain that is itself a fractal, so the arithmetic of the overtones changes. Gray–Scott does it nonlinearly in a system that eats energy to stay standing — and there the pattern begins to move on its own.

The fourth is not an instrument but a brake. Everything here reports a number it predicted before it measured, and is built to be able to fail.
